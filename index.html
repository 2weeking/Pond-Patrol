THE FIELD GUIDE

Welcome to the pond.

Keep balloons from reaching the exit. Place ducks on grass, pop balloons for coins, and invest in upgrades. Survive all 15 waves with at least one life.

🦆 Build your flock

Scout Ducks throw piercing darts. Super Ducks fire rapidly. Mage Ducks cast seeking spells and see camouflaged balloons from the start.

⬆ Make them stronger

Select a placed duck to buy either upgrade path, change its targeting, or sell it for 70% of your investment. Each path has three tiers; you can buy both.

🎈 Read the balloons

Colors are layers: red → blue → green → yellow → pink. Black and white split into pinks. Rainbow balloons split into black and white. Ceramics contain rainbows.

🛡 Special trouble

Lead resists ordinary darts: use a Mage, Scout Shatter Tips, or Super Plasma. Camo needs a Mage, Scout Spotter, or Super Radar. Regrow balloons rebuild lost layers if left alone.

🐋 The big ones

Waves 13–15 introduce Zeppelins, Dreadnoughts, and the Leviathan. Their hulls release smaller threats. Bosses cost many lives if they escape.

⌨ Little shortcuts

1 / 2 / 3: pick duck · click: place/select · Esc: cancel · Space: start/pause · F: speed · arrows: move map cursor · Enter: place/select · S: sell selected duck.
